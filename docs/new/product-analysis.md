# Product Analysis — SOLO

## 1. Product Identity

**Product Name:** SOLO  
**Full Name (System):** Student Management System  
**Tagline:**  
SOLO — مدیریت دانش‌آموزات با ما آسونه

---

## 2. Vision & Mission

SOLO is a multi-role educational management platform designed to connect teachers, students, and parents in a unified ecosystem.

### Mission:

- Simplify private class management for teachers
- Increase transparency between teachers, students, and parents
- Provide structured academic tracking and performance analytics
- Enable online learning workflows for private tutoring environments

---

## 3. Launch Plan

- **Launch Timeline:** 1 month MVP
- **Target Market:** Private tutors and individual teachers
- **Future Expansion:** Schools and institutes (multi-tenant enterprise model)

---

## 4. Key Value Proposition

- Centralized student management system
- Real-time academic tracking for parents
- Automation of scheduling, exams, and assignments
- Teacher-first SaaS with monetization model
- Built-in marketplace for teacher profiles (Next.js public layer)

---

## 5. Product Layers

### Public Layer (Next.js)

- Teacher listing marketplace
- Teacher profile pages (SEO optimized)
- Landing pages

### App Layer (React)

- Teacher dashboard
- Student panel
- Parent panel
- Admin panel

---

## 6. Core Differentiators

- Built specifically for private tutoring ecosystem
- Dual system: SaaS + public SEO marketplace
- Automated academic workflows
- Monetized teacher profiles (Pro system)
- Role-based deep access control
