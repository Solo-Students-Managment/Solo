# Investor Pitch — Student Management System (English)

## Elevator pitch

A modern, scalable student management platform designed for schools and tutoring centers to streamline scheduling, assessments, communication, and revenue tracking.

## Problem

Schools and tutors rely on fragmented tools for scheduling, attendance, exams, and parent communication.

## Solution

An integrated web platform providing session management, exam workflows, parent approvals, messaging, and admin analytics — reducing administrative overhead and improving student outcomes.

## Market opportunity

Target K-12 schools, tutoring centers, and online educators. The TAM includes regional market estimates and pilot customer segments.

## Business model

SaaS subscriptions with tiered pricing for small, medium, and enterprise institutions.

## Traction & roadmap

Pilot-ready MVP, roadmap for integrations (LMS, payment gateways), and internationalization.

## Ask

Seeking seed funding to build integrations, hire engineering and sales, and run pilot programs.
